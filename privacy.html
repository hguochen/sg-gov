Privacy

SG Gov is built so there is nothing to protect: it does not collect, send or store what you type.

What happens to your question

Your question is matched against a list of topics by JavaScript running in your own browser. It is not sent to any server, logged or saved. When you leave or refresh the page, it is gone.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no login, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The site is a set of static files.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s font servers when the page loads. If you follow a link to an official agency site, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Never share sensitive details here

SG Gov will never ask for your NRIC, Singpass password or payment details. Only enter those on official government websites that you reach directly.
