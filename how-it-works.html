SG Gov

How it works

Government services are spread across dozens of agency websites. SG Gov is one place to start: describe what you need, and it points you to the right agency.

  1. You describe what you needType it in your own words, for example “renew my passport” or “check CPF”.
  2. Your words are matched in your browserSG Gov compares your words with a curated list of common topics. This happens on your device and nothing is sent to a server.
  3. You get a short answerThe answer card sums up the usual steps in plain language.
  4. You go to the official sourceEvery answer links to the agency that runs the service. That site is the authority on rules, fees and eligibility.

What SG Gov isn’t

SG Gov is an unofficial demo project. It isn’t run by the Government of Singapore, it doesn’t process applications, and it never asks you to log in. To transact, use the official agency website and log in with Singpass there.
